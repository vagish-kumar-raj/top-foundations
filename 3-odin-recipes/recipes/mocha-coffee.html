Mocha Coffee

Ingredients

  • 1 cup hot brewed coffee
  • 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons milk

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Pour hot coffee into a mug. Stir in cocoa, sugar, and milk. Enjoy!
  3. Tip:It’s easy to personalize this drink with your favorite milk, such as oat, soy, or almond and top it with whipped cream for extra indulgence.
  4. Sit back, relax, and enjoy!
